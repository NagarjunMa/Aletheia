Frequently Asked

Is this allowed by LinkedIn? ↓
Ascendia is a Chrome extension that assists you with drafting. We do not automate browser interactions or scrapings in a way that violates TOS. You remain the sender of every message.
Does it learn my writing style? ↓
Yes! You can upload previous messages or connect your LinkedIn 'Sent' box so Ascendia mirrors your tone, vocabulary, and sign-offs perfectly.
What languages are supported? ↓
We currently support English, Spanish, French, German, and Portuguese with high-fidelity contextual nuances for each.
